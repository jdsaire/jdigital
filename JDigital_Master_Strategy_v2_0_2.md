### 3.4 Contact activation — the playbook *(flags resolved)*

You have **two genuinely warm threads inside BBVA.** This is not cold outreach. The anti-opportunism defense is built into the relationship histories themselves.

**The anti-opportunism principle.** Opportunism is reaching out *only* when you need something, from a transactional baseline. You are not there with either man. The four rules:
1. **Warm the channel before the ask.** You've been silent-liking for months. Start leaving a few *genuine comments* now, so the eventual message isn't the first sound you've made.
2. **Lead with the existing relationship**, not the role.
3. **Make the ask small and easy** — their read, a coffee — never large and transactional ("refer me," "get me hired").
4. **Bring value, not just need** — you already want this; operationalize it per person below.
